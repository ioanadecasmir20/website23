About CDH Security

CDH Security focuses on straightforward, dependable security delivery. We support property managers, venues and businesses who want visible security presence, good communication and officers who understand the environment they are working in.

Our teams are selected for professionalism and attitude as much as experience. We provide clear site instructions, ongoing supervision and practical feedback so that standards stay consistent from the first shift to the last.

Consistent standards

Focus on dress, timekeeping and communication so your staff and visitors know exactly who is in charge of security on site.

Operational focus

Site-specific assignment instructions, simple incident reporting and supervisors who know your locations and people.

Flexible coverage

Options for permanent posts, seasonal peaks, events and emergency cover when your own arrangements change at short notice.

Services

Select a service on the left to see more detail.

Security guarding

Static guarding for offices, industrial sites, residential developments and mixed use premises. Officers control access, monitor activity and provide a visible deterrent against theft, damage and anti-social behaviour.

  • Front of house and gatehouse duties
  • Regular site patrols and checkpoint logging
  • Basic health & safety and fire warden duties by agreement

Door supervision

Professional door supervisors for licensed venues and events. Our priority is customer care, conflict prevention and keeping your premises safe and compliant.

  • ID and entry control in line with venue policy
  • Queue management and customer assistance
  • Incident response and liaison with management and police

CCTV operators

Control-room staff to monitor your CCTV systems, support on-site teams and coordinate responses to incidents as they develop.

  • Live monitoring and incident detection
  • Evidence gathering and incident logs
  • Working alongside guarding and mobile teams

Mobile patrols

Vehicle or foot patrols at agreed times or on a random schedule to deter unwanted activity across multiple sites or wider estates.

  • Lock/unlock routines for buildings and car parks
  • Visible presence at vulnerable times of day
  • Out-of-hours checks for alarms, doors and lighting

Key holding & alarm response

Secure key holding and out-of-hours alarm response so your own staff are not called to site in the middle of the night.

  • Secure storage of keys and access cards
  • Alarm response with incident reporting
  • Liaison with emergency services and call-out contractors

Concierge & reception security

Officers who combine front-of-house duties with security awareness, creating a professional first impression while protecting your building.

  • Visitor management and pass control
  • Switchboard and helpdesk support by agreement
  • Monitoring of access control and building systems
